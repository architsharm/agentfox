# Design and contributor documentation

**Using AgentFox?** The user documentation (install, quickstart, guides, CLI and API reference,
self-hosting) lives on the website at [useagentfox.com/docs](https://useagentfox.com/docs), and its
source is in [`dashboard/app/docs/`](../dashboard/app/docs/). This directory holds the material
behind the product: how it is built, why it is built that way, and how its numbers were measured.

```
docs/
├── getting-started.md   a linear first hour, from install to your own agent governed
├── product-tour.md      every integration surface, commands by task, self-hosting
├── status.md            what is built, partial and absent (generated)
├── architecture/        how the system is put together
├── design/              what it must do, and how we know whether it does
├── evaluation/          how the numbers were measured, and how to read them
├── deployment/          running it yourself, beyond the website's self-host page
└── assets/              images used by the top-level README
```

## design/

| Document | What it is | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| [PRD.md](design/PRD.md) | The single canonical product requirements document: the argument, the product, the plan, and what has shipped since (including monitoring and live probing, §13). Long, and the source of truth for requirement IDs. | Product and engineering leadership. |
| [failure-modes.md](design/failure-modes.md) | How deployed agents actually fail: 57 modes in 8 families plus 5 found by an independent audit, grounded in catalogued incidents, with what covers each. | Anyone building a risk case, and anyone checking ours. |
| [coverage-map.md](design/coverage-map.md) | A second coverage view, built from the architecture of an agentic request rather than from the failure-mode catalogue, so it is not circular. Generated by probe. | Anyone checking for gaps the authors did not think of. |
| [gap-analysis.md](design/gap-analysis.md) | Enterprise readiness and competitive position, audited and severity-ranked, ending with what is still open and what is deferred by decision. | Buyers, and us. |
| [control-catalog.md](design/control-catalog.md) | 43 controls mapped to EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, SOC 2, OWASP LLM and Agentic, and MITRE ATLAS, plus what each mapping does not cover. | Compliance and GRC. Mappings are engineering drafts, not legal advice. |
| [traceability.md](design/traceability.md) | Every requirement mapped to the module that implements it and the test that covers it. | Contributors, and audit. |
| [competitor-analysis.md](design/competitor-analysis.md) | Market landscape, our niche, and where competitors win. The public /compare page cites it. | Anyone comparing options. |
| [architecture-differentiation.md](design/architecture-differentiation.md) | Where each competitor sits in the stack and what it decides on, from their docs and code; our architectural primitives mapped to bound benchmark claims; pitch lines, objections, and the head-to-head runs still missing. | Founders and AEs preparing a technical pitch, and the sceptical buyer checking it. |
| [production-readiness-review.md](design/production-readiness-review.md) | Gaps found re-checking the HLD and LLD against the live code, what has been fixed since, and the settings a production deployment needs (secrets, cron, webhooks, Slack, the showcase flag). | Anyone deciding whether to run it in production. |
| [structure-proposal.md](design/structure-proposal.md) | The planned code restructure: layers, a harness adapter interface, capability packs, the decisions taken and progress per phase. | Contributors planning a large change. |
| [oss-register.md](design/oss-register.md) | Every wrapped open-source project: licence, health, verdict, our exposure, and why some well-known projects are deliberately off the critical path. | Contributors adding a dependency, and licence review. |

The documents that began as PRD appendices keep their letters in their titles and section
numbers (Appendix A to E, §B.6 and so on), because other documents cite them that way.

## evaluation/

| Document | What it is | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| [evidence-standards.md](evaluation/evidence-standards.md) | How to read our numbers: what each kind of evidence establishes and the limits that apply first. | Read this before quoting any benchmark result anywhere. |
| [benchmarking-whitepaper.md](evaluation/benchmarking-whitepaper.md) | The product capability by capability: what it does, how it was measured, every round including the ones that went badly, and how it differs from the market. | Evaluators, and anyone who wants the methodology rather than the headline. |
| [responding-to-the-critique.md](evaluation/responding-to-the-critique.md) | What the strongest public criticism of this category gets right, what it gets wrong, and what we changed because of it. | A reader who has seen the critique and is sceptical, reasonably. |
| [judgment/results.md](evaluation/judgment/results.md) | The study behind the judgment tiers: where a hosted judgment beats our detectors and where it loses. Its headline numbers are bound in `benchmarks/claims.yaml`. | Evaluators who want the evidence, not the summary. |
| [judgment/vs-shipping.md](evaluation/judgment/vs-shipping.md), [cascade.md](evaluation/judgment/cascade.md), [safepyramid.md](evaluation/judgment/safepyramid.md) | Supporting write-ups for that study: against the shipping detectors, the cascade design, and the SafePyramid run. | Evaluators checking a specific result. |
| [dataset-sourcing.md](evaluation/dataset-sourcing.md) | A working record, kept for provenance: research preceding a round of benchmarks, with candidate datasets, verified licences, and rejected candidates with reasons. Not maintained once that work was done. | Anyone checking where a dataset came from. |
| [../benchmarks/README.md](../benchmarks/README.md) | The benchmark index: every harness, its dataset, its licence and how to re-run it. | Anyone reproducing a result. |

Numbers quoted in these documents are bound to result files by
[`benchmarks/claims.yaml`](../benchmarks/claims.yaml); `python scripts/check/claims.py --check` fails if
a quoted figure drifts from its source.
